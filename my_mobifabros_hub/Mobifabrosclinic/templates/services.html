Our Services

At Mobifabros Clinic, we believe that quality healthcare should never feel out of reach. From everyday family check-ups and maternal care to rapid diagnostics and reliable treatments, our comprehensive medical services are designed to keep you and your loved ones healthy and strong. We combine professional expertise with a deep commitment to our neighbors—bringing trusted, compassionate care right where you need it most.

  2. Services

Primary Healthcare & General Consultation

  • General Check-ups: Routine health assessments for individuals and families to monitor well-being, catch issues early, and stay healthy.
  • Treatment of Common Illnesses: Quick, reliable diagnosis and treatment for everyday ailments like malaria, respiratory infections, stomach bugs, and flu.
  • Chronic Disease Management: Continuous, supportive care and monitoring for conditions such as high blood pressure, diabetes, and asthma.

Maternal & Child Health

  • Antenatal & Postnatal Care: Dedicated support and health check-ups for expectant mothers and new babies to ensure safe pregnancies and healthy development.
  • Child Immunizations: Routine vaccinations to protect infants and children against preventable childhood diseases.
  • Growth Monitoring: Regular check-ins on children's development, nutrition advice, and vitamin supplementation.

Diagnostic & Laboratory Services

  • On-Site Testing: Fast, accurate laboratory tests (such as malaria blood smears, typhoid tests, and urinalysis) to help doctors diagnose conditions quickly without unnecessary travel.
  • Basic Screening: pressure checks, blood sugar level testing, and physical assessments.

Community Health & Outreach

  • Mobile Health Camps & Education: Bringing basic health screenings, wellness talks, and preventive care directly to local villages and community groups.
  • Health Education & Counseling: Practical guidance on nutrition, hygiene, sanitation, and family wellness tailored to everyday rural living.
